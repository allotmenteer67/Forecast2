Location, places, units, and which forecasters appear on Compare.

Saved places

Switching to a saved place reuses whatever forecast history it already has, instead of starting fresh. Adding one here doesn't change what's currently shown — use Switch above once it's added, or update the location below to look somewhere without saving it.

A postcode or a plain place name both work — e.g. "TA6" or "Bridgwater".

Units

Each condition can use metric or imperial independently — e.g. mm for Rain alongside mph for Wind.
